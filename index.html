01 — About

Attachment theory as a lens on connection, cognition, and the self.

I study how attachment security shapes the way people understand themselves, explore their world, and grow—and how these processes extend into relationships with AI.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Faculty of Letters, Arts and Sciences at Waseda University in Tokyo. Working across social, personality, and educational psychology, I bring attachment theory into dialogue with mindfulness, self-concept clarity, self-compassion, resilience, and belief in free will.

My current research follows two closely connected directions: attachment-security priming in cognition and social cognition, and AI attachment—the ways people seek reassurance, trust, disclose, and feel connected in interactions with generative AI. I use cross-sectional, longitudinal, weekly diary, dyadic, and scale-development designs to connect careful psychological measurement with questions that matter in everyday life.

Research community & public engagement

Beyond research, I serve on the Public Relations Committee of the Japanese Psychological Association and review manuscripts for international psychology journals. My research on attachment and human–AI relationships has been introduced to audiences across several countries and languages.

02 — Research

Three connected lines of inquiry.

From established close relationships to emerging relationships with intelligent systems.

01

Human–AI attachment

Applying attachment theory to understand how people seek support from, trust, disclose to, and form emotional bonds with generative AI.

  • Scale development
  • AI attachment
  • Personality inference
02

Attachment security & cognition

Examining how attachment security relates to exploration, self-knowledge, efficacy, thinking style, and social cognition over time.

  • Security priming
  • Longitudinal models
  • Self-concept
03

Mindfulness, self & resilience

Investigating mindfulness as a psychological process linking secure attachment with clarity, resilience, self-compassion, and growth.

  • Mindfulness
  • Resilience
  • Personal growth
