01 — About

Research at the meeting point of relationships and the mind.

My work asks a simple question with far-reaching implications: What helps people feel secure enough to understand themselves, explore, and grow?

I approach this question through attachment theory and study its connections with mindfulness, self-concept clarity, resilience, and personal growth. More recently, I have extended this perspective to human–AI relationships—examining how people form emotional bonds with generative AI and how those bonds shape interaction and self-understanding.

I use cross-sectional, longitudinal, diary, dyadic, and scale-development methods. Across these approaches, my aim is to connect careful psychological measurement with questions that matter in everyday life.

02 — Research

Three connected lines of inquiry.

From established close relationships to emerging relationships with intelligent systems.

01

Human–AI attachment

Applying attachment theory to understand how people seek support from, trust, disclose to, and form emotional bonds with generative AI.

  • Scale development
  • AI attachment
  • Personality inference
02

Attachment security & cognition

Examining how attachment security relates to exploration, self-knowledge, efficacy, thinking style, and social cognition over time.

  • Security priming
  • Longitudinal models
  • Self-concept
03

Mindfulness, self & resilience

Investigating mindfulness as a psychological process linking secure attachment with clarity, resilience, self-compassion, and growth.

  • Mindfulness
  • Resilience
  • Personal growth
