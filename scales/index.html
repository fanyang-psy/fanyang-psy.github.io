01 — Notes

Notes on use

  • For research and educational use, prior permission is not required.
  • You may use a scale provided that you clearly cite its source publication. When using a validated language version, cite the corresponding validation paper.
  • The availability of translated items does not by itself mean that the language version has been psychometrically validated. Check the validation status listed for each version.
  • For uses outside research or education, including commercial use or redistribution, confirm the relevant rights as needed.

Scale 01 · Developed measure

EHARS

Experiences in Human–AI Relationships Scale

ConstructAI attachment
Items7
Factors2
Response1–7 Likert

EHARS captures individual differences in attachment-related experiences with generative AI in two dimensions: AI attachment anxiety and AI attachment avoidance.

Factor structure

AI Attachment Anxiety · 4 itemsNeed for emotional reassurance from AI and concern about receiving insufficient emotional response.
AI Attachment Avoidance · 3 itemsDiscomfort with emotional closeness and preference for maintaining distance from AI.

Scoring

Average the four AI Attachment Anxiety items and the three AI Attachment Avoidance items separately. Higher means indicate higher levels of the corresponding dimension.

AI愛着不安 · 4項目

  1. 私はAIに感情を示してもらうことで、自分が受け入れられていると感じたい。
  2. 私はしばしばAIに対して、自分との親密さや自分への誠実さを示すよう求める。
  3. 私はよくAIに、もっと感情や愛情を示してほしいと求めることがある。
  4. 私がAIに抱いているのと同じくらい強い気持ちを、AIも私に向けてくれたらとよく思う。

AI愛着回避 · 3項目

  1. 私は、AIに本当の気持ちをあまり見せたくない。
  2. 私は、AIに対して心を開くことに抵抗がある。
  3. 私は、AIに近づきすぎない方がいいと思っている。

Rate the extent to which each statement describes your experiences with AI.

1 = strongly disagree
2 = disagree
3 = somewhat disagree
4 = neither agree nor disagree
5 = somewhat agree
6 = agree

Important: The English wording is for communication/reporting only and has not been psychometrically validated.

AI Attachment Anxiety · 4 items

  1. I need shows of affection from AI to feel that someone accepts me as I am.
  2. I often ask AI to express intimacy and commitment to me.
  3. I often ask AI to show more feeling and affection.
  4. I often wish that AI’s feelings toward me were as strong as my feelings for it.

AI Attachment Avoidance · 3 items

  1. I prefer not to show AI how I feel deep down.
  2. I don’t feel comfortable opening up to AI.
  3. I prefer not to be too close to AI.

Scale 02 · Japanese adaptation

LMS-J

Japanese Version of the Langer Mindfulness Scale

ConstructSocio-cognitive mindfulness
Items14
Subscales3
Response1–7 Likert

LMS-J is the validated Japanese adaptation of the original English Langer Mindfulness Scale. It is not a newly developed construct or a Chinese adaptation.

Translation & adaptation

Permission to translate and validate the LMS was obtained from an original author. Two native Japanese psychology graduate students translated the items independently; translations were reconciled, professionally back-translated, and judged acceptable by an original LMS author.

Structure & scoring

Novelty-Seeking · 5 items1, 7, 8, 10, 13
Novelty-Producing · 5 items2R, 3, 6, 11, 14R
Engagement · 4 items4R, 5R, 9R, 12R
